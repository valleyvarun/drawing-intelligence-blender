These are original VCO triangle assets. The application reads their
coordinates and colors directly; no icon geometry has been redrawn.

Shape: app.shape.dat, 3D Printer: app.printer.dat, 3D Extruder:
app.extruder.dat, and Sketch Mesh: app.sketch.dat are not Blender icons.
scripts/build_app_icons.py writes them in the color of
ops.gpencil.primitive_box.dat; the Shape icon also reuses its "+"
triangles and line width for square, circle, and triangle outlines,
and the Sketch Mesh icon uses that line width.
